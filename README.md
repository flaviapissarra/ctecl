## About

CTeCL is a personal research lab focused on the intersection of **Computational Linguistics**, **Generative AI**, and **Software Engineering**. It started as an extensionist project in the Letters course and now I run it as my personal lab. Current areas of interest include:

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Prompt Optimization
- AI Agents & Autonomous Verification Systems
- Privacy-First NLP Applications
- Business Intelligence enhanced by Large Language Models
